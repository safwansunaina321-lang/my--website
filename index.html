🤖 What is Robotics?

Robotics is the technology of designing, building, and programming robots to perform useful tasks.

🧠 Robot Brain

A controller such as Arduino can act as the brain of a robot and control its components.

📡 Sensors

Sensors help robots detect things around them, such as distance, light, temperature, and objects.

🦾 Motors

Motors help robots move. Servo and DC motors are common examples.

⚡ Arduino

Arduino is a beginner-friendly platform for learning electronics and robotics programming.

💡 Electronics

Learn about LEDs, resistors, buzzers, switches, breadboards, batteries, and wires.

💻 Programming

Programming gives robots instructions using variables, conditions, loops, and functions.

🚀 Projects

Build beginner projects such as distance detectors, automatic lights, and servo systems.
